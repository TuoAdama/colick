# Prompts des cinq maquettes minimalistes

## 06-editorial

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Create ONE extremely restrained, professionally typeset mobile web UI screenshot for Coliclic, a parcel transport app. Portrait 1024x1536. Flat UI fills canvas. No phone frame, mockup backdrop, shadows, gradients, illustrations, maps, airplanes, luggage icons, confetti, giant checkmarks, decorative blobs, ornamental branding or giant rounded cards. NO DARK BLUE ANYWHERE except the small official Facebook logo. Typography should look like plain Inter with modest weights, charcoal #292722, muted gray supporting text. Main background warm white #FAF9F6. Use credible mobile proportions equivalent to 390px CSS viewport. Refined spacing, extremely clean alignment. Title at most 26px equivalent. Thin 1px rules. Body 14px equivalent. All UI text French, clear and accurately spelled.
This is the recap page immediately after creation of a trip. Exactly these details visible: small plain wordmark “coliclic”, discreet text “Trajet publié”, title “Votre trajet”, origin “Paris”, destination “Abidjan”, departure label “Départ” with “15 déc. 2026 · 10 h 30”, arrival label “Arrivée” with “15 déc. 2026 · 16 h 05”, “Rendez-vous” with “Aéroport Paris–Charles de Gaulle”, “Capacité” with “12 kg”, “Disponible” with “12 kg”, “Tarif” with “10 €/kg”, “Réservation” with “Sur approbation”.
A single modest primary button “Partager” with a simple export symbol MUST be directly adjacent horizontally to three small recognizable official logos of WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, in that order. Logos should be 20px equivalent, not oversized. Small helper “Créer une image du trajet” under this row. A quiet footer link “Mes trajets”. All data and share row comfortably fit one viewport. No extra text, invented functionality, secondary CTAs, or filler. This should look like a carefully coded minimal app screen, not an infographic or promotional poster.
Direction: Editorial typography. Pure white. Left-aligned header. Simple origin and destination stacked in one unboxed section, separated by a short vertical hairline, without icons. Details in tidy full-width label/value rows. Charcoal filled rectangular button, 6px radius. Only accent is a tiny muted sage status dot. Strong white space. Keep data rows easy to scan.
```
