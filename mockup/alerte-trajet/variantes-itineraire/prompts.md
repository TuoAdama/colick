# Prompts ImageGen — variantes de l’itinéraire illustré

Les cinq maquettes utilisent l’outil ImageGen intégré et la [maquette de référence](../02-itineraire-illustre.png).

## Carte de quartier

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image 1 is the exact Coliclic desktop landing mockup to edit. Output one landscape 1536 × 1024 screenshot. Redesign only the right-hand route-and-email illustration in the white center section. Preserve the left column position, generous whitespace, typography, all text verbatim, orange button and page crop, including top video controls and bottom heading. Left copy must read exactly “Vous ne trouvez pas de trajet ?”, “Créez une alerte pour être prévenu par e-mail dès qu’un trajet correspondant est disponible.”, “Rechercher un trajet”. Bottom heading “Votre sécurité, notre priorité”. Match the screenshot scale. Use brand navy #023047, warm orange #FB8500, cream #FFF5E9, white, and subtle #219EBC only if appropriate. Do not add labels, badges, captions, watermarks, device frames or controls. Keep the whole illustration within the right column, with similar visual weight to the reference.
Create a light cream flat map tile with rounded rectangle silhouette and a few barely visible cream-colored street lines, no place labels. Two navy map pins connect diagonally via a precise orange dotted route; attach a small white envelope tile with navy outline to the destination. Crisp simple vector-like style.
```
